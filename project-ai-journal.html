01The idea

The most private thing you write shouldn’t be the first thing you upload.

Typical AI journal

Your entry makes a round trip.

To get a summary or a mood, the words leave the device, get processed on a server you don’t control, and come back.

04What it does

Four things, all of them local.

Privacy first

All AI processing happens on the device. No account, no upload, nothing sent to the cloud.

A local language model

Powered by Apple’s MLX framework, built for efficient machine learning on Apple silicon.

Smart insights

Summaries and gentle reflections generated from your own entries, not from a template.

Mood tracking

The sentiment of what you write, tracked over time, so the pattern shows up before you name it.

05How it works

Inference, in your hands.

MLX runs the model in the iPad’s unified memory, so the same chip that draws the interface also does the thinking — no round trip, no waiting on a connection.
